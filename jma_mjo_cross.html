Three- and seven-day running mean values are used.
The base period for the normal is 1991-2020.

'Hist', 'Anom' and 'Norm' are defined as below:
- Hist: Historical data (raw data)
- Anom: Anomaly (i.e., deviation from the climatological normal)
- Norm: Climatological normal data (i.e., the 1991 - 2020 average).
